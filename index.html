Frequently Asked Questions

What counts as a dental emergency?

Severe toothache, facial swelling, a knocked-out or cracked tooth, bleeding that will not stop and a painful abscess are all reasons to call right away.


Can I be seen today?

Yes. We offer same-day appointments and are open 24/7, so call 470-298-8207 and we will tell you when to come in.


What should I do if a tooth is knocked out?

Pick it up by the crown, rinse it gently without scrubbing, keep it moist in milk or in your cheek and call us immediately.


What payment methods do you accept?

We accept cash and credit cards, and we explain the cost of treatment before it begins.

Severe Toothache Relief

A severe toothache is your body telling you something is wrong, and guessing at the cause only prolongs the pain. We examine the tooth, take any X-rays needed and identify whether the problem is decay, infection, a cracked tooth or a gum issue.

Once we know the source, we relieve the pain and explain the treatment that will fix it for good. You leave knowing what happened, what comes next and how to stay comfortable at home.

Emergency Tooth Extraction

Some teeth are too broken, loose or infected to save, and removing them promptly protects the rest of your mouth. We use effective numbing so the extraction is as comfortable as possible.

Afterward we give you simple aftercare steps, pain management advice and options for replacing the tooth later, so healing goes smoothly and your bite stays healthy.

Same-Day Root Canal

When infection reaches the nerve of a tooth, pain and swelling often follow. A root canal removes the damaged tissue, cleans the inside of the tooth and seals it so you can keep your natural tooth.

We offer urgent root canal treatment so you do not have to endure days of pain waiting for a slot. Modern techniques make the procedure far more comfortable than most people expect.

Broken, Chipped or Knocked-Out Teeth

Falls, sports and accidents can crack, chip or knock out a tooth in a moment. Acting quickly gives the best chance of saving it, so call us right away and tell us what happened.

We can bond or restore chipped teeth, stabilize cracked ones and re-implant a knocked-out tooth when possible. We also check the gums and nearby teeth for hidden damage.
